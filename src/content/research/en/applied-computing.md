Applied computing connects software engineering with needs in other fields. Each project should clearly define its domain, question, method, and how the technological contribution will be evaluated.

This area also allows emerging technologies to be studied without assuming their relevance in advance: they are incorporated when they make a verifiable contribution to a real problem and an interdisciplinary research process.
