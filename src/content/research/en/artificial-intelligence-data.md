Artificial intelligence and data analytics are treated as cross-cutting technological capabilities, not as BIGATIC's sole focus. Their use should respond to a research need and explicit evaluation criteria.

Projects may use analysis methods, machine learning, automation, or intelligent systems when these offer a relevant way to study or address the problem at hand.
