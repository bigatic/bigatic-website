Cybersecurity is approached as a property of systems and their development processes. The focus is on understanding risk, integrating verifiable controls, and evaluating technical decisions from early stages.

Depending on the research problem, projects may examine application security, vulnerability analysis, incident response, automation, and engineering practices intended to reduce preventable failures.
