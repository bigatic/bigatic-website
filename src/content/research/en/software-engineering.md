This area organizes questions and projects concerned with the systematic engineering of software. It considers problem analysis, architectural decisions, implementation, verification, and the evolution of computing solutions.

Its scope may include requirements practices, quality, testing, continuous integration and delivery, distributed systems, and developer tooling. Specific topics are defined by each project and do not imply published activity across every subject listed here.
