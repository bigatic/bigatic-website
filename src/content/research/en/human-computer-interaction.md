Human-Computer Interaction studies the relationship between people and digital systems. At BIGATIC, it supports the formulation and evaluation of solutions in terms of context of use, accessibility, comprehensibility, and interaction quality.

Specific methods depend on each project and may include user research, prototyping, usability evaluation, interaction design, and accessibility review.
