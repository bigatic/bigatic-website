## Education and focus

An eighth semester Software Engineering student at Universidad de Santander. His area of specialization is interface design and user experience. In the BIGATIC research group he joins the Human-Computer Interaction and Emerging Technologies strand, where he works on ADHD-UX, a design system aimed at supporting attention and planning in young people with ADHD. He is interested in bringing design judgment to a project that connects engineering with psychology.
