## Education and experience

Andrés David Gómez Morales is a sixth-semester Software Engineering student at Universidad de Santander. He is currently on academic exchange at Riga Technical University in Latvia and works as a research assistant at BIOCOM under the supervision of Dr. Efraín Pinzón.

## Research focus

His work connects software development with the life sciences. His interests include computational biology, structural bioinformatics, protein folding, molecular docking, and biomolecular simulation. On the computing side, he focuses on scientific software, reproducible workflows, infrastructure for computational workloads, and the security of research systems and data.
