BIGATIC invites Software Engineering students from the third semester onward and students from other UDES programs taking part in interdisciplinary projects.

The group meets in person every week. No prior experience is required; commitment, curiosity, and initiative are expected. Applications are open through **Friday, August 14, 2026**.

Complete details, research topics, and the official application link are available on the [call page](/en/calls/call-2026b/).
